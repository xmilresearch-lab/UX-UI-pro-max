The problem

Your task list is a source of stress, not focus.

Most tools dump everything into one massive list and let you drown. Focusly is different — it surfaces only what you should work on right now.

Endless context switching

Jumping between apps kills your deep work. The average knowledge worker switches tools 300+ times a day.

Backlogs that never shrink

You add tasks faster than you complete them. The list grows until you stop trusting your own system.

No sense of priority

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Focusly is intentionally minimal. Every feature earns its place by helping you do more focused work.

AI Daily Priorities

Focusly learns your patterns and surfaces your top 3–5 tasks each morning — so you always know where to start.

Focus Timer

Built-in Pomodoro-style timer locks you into deep work. Distraction-free mode blocks notifications automatically.

Smart Inbox

Capture tasks from anywhere — email, Slack, notes — and Focusly files them automatically. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Team Collaboration

Share projects, delegate tasks, and see what your team is focused on — without the overhead of a full PM tool.

Weekly Review

A guided Friday ritual surfaces your wins, clears cruft, and preps your next week — all in under 10 minutes.

100+ Integrations

Connect your tools — GitHub, Notion, Calendar, Jira — and pull tasks where they live without copy-pasting.
